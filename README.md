Privacy Policy

LiftCare is a business application for elevator maintenance, repair, equipment, site, customer, activity, attachment, and notification management.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when users access and use the LiftCare mobile application.

Information We Collect

When you use LiftCare, we may collect and process the following information:

- Account information, such as name, email address, user ID, company/database code, role, and permission information.
- Business operation data, such as maintenance requests, repair orders, equipment information, site information, customer information, work activities, and related status updates.
- User-provided content, such as photos, files, documents, notes, messages, and attachments uploaded to maintenance or repair records.
- Device and notification information, such as device identifier, platform, app version, and push notification token used to deliver work-related notifications.
- Technical information necessary to operate the service, such as login session data and server communication logs.

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

- Authenticate users and provide access to the LiftCare system.
- Display and manage maintenance requests, repair orders, equipment, sites, customers, activities, attachments, and notifications.
- Allow users to upload photos, files, and documents related to maintenance and repair work.
- Send push notifications about maintenance, repair, and work activity updates.
- Improve reliability, security, and operation of the application.
- Provide technical support and resolve user-reported issues.
- Comply with legal, security, and business obligations.

Camera, Photo Library, and File Access

LiftCare may request access to the camera to allow users to take photos for maintenance or repair records.

LiftCare may request access to the photo library to allow users to select existing images for upload.

LiftCare may allow users to select files or documents to attach to maintenance or repair records.

These permissions are used only for features initiated by the user.

Push Notifications

LiftCare uses push notifications to inform users about work-related updates, including maintenance requests, repair orders, and activity changes.

We may store a device push notification token for this purpose.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users’ personal information.

We may share or process information with service providers necessary to operate the application, such as hosting providers, backend systems, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification service, and related infrastructure providers.

These services are used only to provide and maintain LiftCare functionality.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. Communication between the app and server is transmitted over secure HTTPS connections where applicable.

User credentials and session tokens are stored securely on the device using platform secure storage mechanisms.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide LiftCare services, support business operations, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

Account Access and Deletion

LiftCare accounts are managed by the organization or system administrator. Users who need to update, deactivate, or delete account-related information should contact their organization administrator or support contact.

Children’s Privacy

LiftCare is intended for business use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
